About

Hello! I am Hakan Bora Yavuzkara, a physics and computer science double major at Davidson College. I am currently a visiting student at Oxford University for my junior year. I plan to pursue a PhD in computational physics. My current research involves building a foundation model for time projection chambers under the supervision of Dr. Michelle Kuchera and Dr. Raghu Ramanujan at the ALPhA group at Davidson College. I have also recently started getting interested in DFT, MLIP, and MBPT. I find these fields exciting, and I'm looking forward to exploring them more through studying and research.

Research

  • Transformers for Track Completion: During the summer of my freshman year, I did research with the ALPhA group. I worked on implementing the transformer-based neural network AdaPoinTr as a transformer-based neural network for completing the broken tracks of Activate Target Time Projection Chamber (AT-TPC) data. I trained on artifically broken simulated (16O) + α data, and tested on experimental (16O) + α data.
  • TPCNet Foundation Model: During the summer of my sophomore year, I did research with the ALPhA group. I worked on developing a foundation model for the AT-TPC using self-supervised learning, by having the model unscramble AT-TPC events as if it was solving a jigsaw puzzle. I compared this approach against training from scratch and contrastive learning.
